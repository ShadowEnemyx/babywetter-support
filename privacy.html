Babywetter – Privacy Policy

1. No accounts, no tracking

Babywetter needs no user account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking services.

2. Data on your device

Birth date, premature-birth flag, stroller, gear and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to the developer or third parties. You can delete all data in the app under “Info”.

3. Location

If you allow it, the app determines your approximate location while it is open. These coordinates are sent to the weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch the weather. No names, device identifiers or data about your child are sent. The developer does not store your location; the device only briefly caches the last weather result.

4. Open-Meteo

For technical reasons your device's IP address may be visible to the weather service. See open-meteo.com/en/terms.

5. Your rights

The developer neither receives nor stores personal data about you, so there is nothing to hand out or delete on their side. You delete local data in the app.

6. Developer and contact
