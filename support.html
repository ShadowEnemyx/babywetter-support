Babywetter – Support

Babywetter shows what your baby can wear for a stroller walk, based on weather, age, stroller and gear.

Help and contact

Questions, bugs or suggestions: open a request on GitHub. Please do not post personal data about your child.

FAQ

The app cannot find weather. Check your internet connection and location permission (Settings › Privacy › Location Services › Babywetter). Without weather data you can set the temperature yourself in the app.

How do I delete my data? In the app under “Info” › “Delete all data”.

Important

Babywetter is a guide and does not replace advice from a midwife or pediatrician. Temperature thresholds are estimates. Always check your baby's neck.
